STANDARD, CONDENSED

- Three types: Signal 500-700 (one development, what it changes for a buyer), Profile 1,200-1,800, Verdict 1,800-2,600.
- Voice: active, concrete, one idea per sentence, no hype vocabulary (revolutionary, cutting-edge, seamless, leverage...), no AI tells, expand abbreviations once, admit at least one limit in every Profile.
- Research: a saved research pack per article; specs from the manufacturer's own page linked inline; price always with currency and "as of Month YYYY"; vendor claims labelled as claims, never as measured results; two independent sources beyond the vendor; never invent market sizes, customer names or test results; no "we tested it" unless we did.
- Linking: 3-6 external links inline, 3-5 links to our own product pages, 2 sibling articles; never link a competitor product page from a Profile.
- Images: one hero cover plus 2-3 inline figures for a Profile, real product photography first, cut-outs only for covers, rights recorded, no AI generation.
- Freshness: "Last checked" line per Profile, quarterly re-verification of price and two headline specs.
