01 — Logo

The Wordmark

The Robotist wordmark is set exclusively in Poppins Medium at 0.20em letter-spacing, all-caps. It is the primary brand identifier and must never be altered, redrawn, or substituted.

Clearspace

Always maintain a minimum clearspace equal to the cap-height of the wordmark on all four sides. This zone must remain free of all other graphic elements, text, and imagery.

06 — Voice

Voice & Tone

Robotist speaks with the confidence of a precision instrument. The voice is direct, considered, and never performative. Every word earns its place.

01

Cold

Unemotional precision. We do not inflate or over-promise. We state facts with clarity and let the work speak for itself.

02

Precise

Every word is deliberate. We favour the specific over the general, the concrete over the abstract. Vague language is a failure of craft.

03

Infinite

We think in systems, not moments. Our language reflects long-term thinking — expansive, forward-looking, and without artificial limits.

Do
  • Write in short, declarative sentences. State what is true.
  • Use active voice. "Robotist processes data in real time." Not "Data is processed."
  • Be specific. "Latency under 12ms" beats "ultra-fast performance."
  • Speak to capability, not aspiration. Show, don't promise.
  • Use technical language accurately when the audience warrants it.
  • Let silence work. White space in copy is as important as white space in design.
Don't
  • Use superlatives without evidence. "World-class," "best-in-class," "revolutionary."
  • Pad sentences with filler. "In order to," "it is important to note that."
  • Use exclamation marks. Enthusiasm is expressed through precision, not punctuation.
  • Anthropomorphise the product unnecessarily. We are a tool, not a companion.
  • Use jargon to impress. Clarity is always the goal.
  • Write in passive voice. It dilutes accountability and authority.

Writing in Practice

Not This

"Our revolutionary AI-powered platform leverages cutting-edge technology to deliver world-class automation solutions that will transform your business!"

This

"Robotist automates complex workflows with sub-second response times. No configuration required."

Not This

"We're super excited to announce our amazing new feature that you're going to absolutely love!"

This

"New: parallel task execution. Up to 8× throughput on multi-step pipelines."

07 — Motion

Motion & Animation

Motion in the Robotist system is purposeful and restrained. Transitions communicate state changes, not personality. Fast, precise, and never decorative.

Primary Easing

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)

A deceleration curve that starts fast and settles with precision. Used for all enter and appear transitions.

Duration — Micro

160ms

Immediate feedback: button presses, toggle switches, focus rings. Imperceptible lag.

Duration — Default

320ms

Standard transition for UI state changes, hover effects, and interactive feedback.

Duration — Page

600ms

Page-level transitions, modal entrances, and full-screen overlays. Deliberate and measured.

Scroll Reveal

opacity + translateY(24px)

Elements enter from 24px below at 0 opacity. Staggered by 80ms per element in groups.

Hover Lift

translateY(−2px) + shadow

Cards and interactive surfaces lift 2px on hover. Shadow deepens to signal interactivity.

Imagery Direction

Robotist imagery favours depth of field and controlled lighting over wide-angle drama. Monochromatic or cool-toned palettes. Technical, precise, and purposeful.

Hero / Feature Image Dark · Technical · Precise

Tone

Dark backgrounds. Cool, desaturated colour grading. Avoid warm tones, bright whites, or high-saturation colour.

Subject

Technology, precision engineering, data, abstract systems. Human subjects should be purposeful and unposed.

Composition

Asymmetric. Generous negative space. Allow the subject to breathe. Avoid busy or cluttered frames.

Treatment

A subtle blue-teal colour grade aligning to the Ocean Blue accent is acceptable. Avoid filters that warm or yellow the image.
