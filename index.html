How deployment works

A clear process from first inquiry to operational robot — managed end-to-end by our Sydney team.

01

Tell us your site and use case

Share your operational environment, goals, and any constraints. We'll ask the right questions to narrow the field.

02

Based on your requirements, we recommend 2–3 machines with clear trade-offs across capability, cost, and deployment complexity.

03

Direct factory confirmation of specifications, lead times, and configuration options — no third-party reseller guesswork.

What Robotist does

Robotist is a global robotics platform that supplies commercial robots for purchase and rental to hotels, hospitals, logistics operators and facilities teams. It sources machines directly from Chinese manufacturers, publishes list prices in USD in a public catalogue, and manages the deployment path end to end: shortlisting, supplier specification checks, import compliance, site assessment, installation, staff training and after-sales support.

  • A global robotics platform connecting businesses with commercial robots for purchase, rental and deployment.
  • Machines across five categories — humanoid, quadruped, cleaning, industrial and companion.
  • USD list prices published on each product page, from USD 5,000 to USD 333,000.
  • Robots are quoted on a WDP (wholesale/dealer price) basis.
  • Direct sourcing from Chinese manufacturers, with a 40+ partner ecosystem.
  • Sourcing, import compliance, site assessment, installation, staff training and maintenance handled end to end.
  • A six-step buyer journey from first inquiry to an operational robot.
  • Three after-sales support tiers: Essential (24-hour response), Priority (4–8 hours) and Premium Command (same day or under 4 hours).
  • A 12-month maintenance and support arrangement is included with each purchase or rental.
  • A public comparison tool evaluates robots side by side on specifications and deployment fit.
  • Operations in Shanghai, Hangzhou and Sydney — shipping worldwide.
  • Deployed in hospitality, healthcare, security, logistics, agriculture, commercial cleaning and live performance.
