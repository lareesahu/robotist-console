INDEPENDENT, BY DESIGN

Project by project.
On agreed terms.

GRN membership is not employment and does not guarantee hours, projects or income. Relevant paid opportunities may be offered when they arise.

Scope, fees, hours, deliverables and expenses are agreed separately before each confirmed assignment. You choose whether an opportunity fits your expertise and availability.

A fuller collaboration agreement covers professional responsibilities and confidentiality before work begins. This page and application are introductions, not a contract.

WORKING TOGETHER

Clear expectations.
Mutual respect.

Your contribution

Keep your skills, experience and availability accurate. Accept only work you are qualified and available to perform, and communicate professionally with Robotist, customers and suppliers. Only make quotes, commitments or promises on Robotist’s behalf when specifically authorized.

Our commitment

We consider specialists for relevant opportunities, define the scope and commercial terms before confirmed work begins, and provide reasonable project information, coordination and access. You remain independent and free to accept or decline each opportunity.

Professional trust

Keep shared customer, supplier, pricing, technical and business information confidential, and use it only for the agreed purpose. Opportunities introduced through Robotist should remain coordinated through Robotist.

A separate written collaboration agreement covers profile permissions, confidentiality, intellectual property, non-circumvention and project engagement and payment terms. Applying or being approved does not sign that agreement or create employment, a partnership, an agency relationship or guaranteed work.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

A few good
questions.

Who is GRN for?

Independent robotics engineers, software developers, integration specialists and technical professionals with practical experience. Tell us what you do, the projects you have worked on and where you can contribute.

Does joining guarantee paid work?

No. We may offer relevant paid opportunities when projects arise. Membership is not employment and does not guarantee projects, hours or income.

Do I have to accept every project?

No. You decide whether each opportunity fits. The scope, deliverables, timing, fees and expenses are agreed separately before an assignment begins.

Will my application become public?

No. Your application and contact details stay private. A profile is prepared separately. We record your approval of the actual profile and photograph before publication, including whether it may be used on our website, in proposals or in capability materials.
