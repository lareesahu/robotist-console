Why This Robot

The ideal platform for robotics education.

Open SDK & Curriculum
Full-featured open SDK with Python, C++, and ROS interfaces. Includes sample projects and learning modules for rapid integration into university courses.
13-DOF Dynamic Motion
13 independently controlled joints allow students to explore gait planning, dynamic locomotion, and sensorimotor control in a hands-on learning environment.
Compact & Classroom-Ready
Lightweight and portable design fits standard classroom and lab environments. Safe for student interaction with built-in collision detection and torque limiting.
Robotist-Managed Deployment
We handle compliance, customs, site assessment, on-site setup, staff training, and 12-month maintenance.
