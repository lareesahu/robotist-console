Why This Robot

A humanoid platform that scales with your ambition.

Three Tiers, One Platform
Start with Lite for accessible service robotics, scale to Pro for enhanced perception and voice interaction, or go Ultra for maximum compute and dexterous five-finger manipulation. The same Wanda architecture across all three — your software investment carries forward.
Bionic Arm Precision
8 degrees of freedom per arm with 982 mm reach and up to 12 kg dual-arm payload on Pro and Ultra. The 2-finger adaptive gripper handles everyday objects; Ultra can also be configured with an optional five-finger dexterous hand for finer manipulation.
Edge AI on NVIDIA Jetson
Jetson Orin NX (70–100 TOPS) on Lite and Pro supports real-time perception and navigation. Ultra's Jetson AGX Orin 64G delivers 275 TOPS for more demanding multimodal inference, manipulation, and planning workloads.
Rich Perception Stack
All variants include an HD stereo head camera. Pro and Ultra add an Orbbec Gemini 2 head camera, one LiDAR, and an Orbbec 336 RGB-D camera for navigation and close-range perception.
VR Teleoperation Ready
Quest 3S integration on all variants enables immersive first-person teleoperation. Operators can work through the robot's cameras and control its arms remotely for inspection, supervised operation, and demonstration-data collection.
Applications

Built for real-world deployment

🏭
Light Manufacturing
Bin picking, kitting, assembly assistance, and material handling. Wanda's 8-DoF arms, 982 mm reach, and up to 12 kg dual-arm payload suit supervised collaborative workflows.
🏥
Healthcare & Aged Care
Patient assistance, medication delivery, and companionship. Voice interaction via DeepSeek multi-LLM enables natural conversation on Pro and Ultra.
🛒
Retail & Hospitality
Customer guidance, inventory management, and in-store service. The Lite variant offers an accessible entry point for retail deployments.
🔬
AI Research
Embodied AI, imitation learning, and sim-to-real transfer. VR teleop captures demonstration data, while AGX Orin on Ultra runs RL policies onboard.
🏗️
Construction & Inspection
Remote site inspection via VR teleop, materials transport, and safety monitoring. Dual LiDAR on Pro/Ultra enables robust operation in unstructured environments.
🎓
Education & Training
Robotics labs, vocational training, and STEM outreach. Wanda's tiered platform lets institutions start with Lite and upgrade as curricula advance.
